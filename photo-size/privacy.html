Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Photo Size processes the images you select on your device. Verlinq Labs does not operate a server that receives or stores those selected photos.

Who we are

Photo Size is an Android utility published by Verlinq Labs, an independent software publisher. Contact: verlinqlabs@gmail.com.

Photos you select

Photo Size uses Android’s system photo picker. Selected images are processed locally for compression, resizing, cropping, previewing, saving, and sharing. Verlinq Labs does not upload those selected photos to a Verlinq Labs server and does not require an account.

Saving and sharing

Saved outputs are written to your device. If you choose Share, Android lets you choose the receiving app or service, which then handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Advertising

The free version may use Google Mobile Ads. According to Google’s documentation, the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share technical data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, including IP address (which may estimate general location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers where available. Verlinq Labs does not use your selected photo itself as advertising data.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Photo Size uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent. Available choices can vary by region and current Google consent requirements. When required, the app can provide an entry point for reviewing or changing advertising privacy choices.

Location

Photo Size does not request Android location permission for its photo-compression features. Advertising technology may use an IP address to estimate a general location.

Accounts

Photo Size does not require a Verlinq Labs account and does not maintain a user profile containing your selected photos.

Children

Photo Size is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Security

Photo processing is designed to occur locally. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Third-party services

Photo Size currently uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent management. Their processing is governed by Google’s applicable terms and privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, advertising configuration, legal requirements, or third-party SDK behavior changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
